Masla

Aap ne mobile qiston par becha, customer ne qist rok di — aur phone phir bhi poora chal raha hai. Paise wapas lena mushkil, phone wapas lena us se bhi mushkil. Har mahine ki isi tension se dukaan ka munafa khatam ho jata hai.

Hal — Installock kya hai?

Installock aik khaas mobile app hai jo qiston par beche gaye phone ki hifazat karti hai. Qist ki tareekh guzarne par phone ke features khud-ba-khud mehdood ho jate hain — aur qist milte hi phone dobara poora khul jata hai. Na jhagra, na peechay bhagna.

Kaise kaam karta hai? (4 aasan steps)

  1. Setup: Phone par QR scan kar ke Installock lagaya jata hai — chand minute ka kaam.
  2. Plan darj: Dashboard par mobile ki qeemat, advance aur mahana qist darj karein.
  3. Auto-limit: Qist ki tareekh guzarne par phone ke features khud mehdood ho jate hain.
  4. Unlock: Qist milte hi dukaan se aik tap par phone dobara poora khul jata hai.

Ahem Features

  • Due-date auto-limit: Tareekh guzarne par phone khud mehdood — yaad-dahani ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Remote Lock / Unlock: Dukaan se door baithe phone lock ya unlock karein.
  • Offline unlock: Shop PIN, Master PIN ya SMS command se — bina net ke bhi.
  • SIM-change alert: Customer SIM badle to dukaan ko foran khabar.
  • Payment reminders: Qist ki tareekh se pehle customer ko yaad-dahani.
  • Factory-reset protection: Reset karne ke bawajood phone dukaan ke control se bahar nahi.
  • FRP protection (option): Phone par sirf dukaan ka account — mukammal hifazat.
  • IMEI capture: Manual ya barcode scan se — har phone ka record mehfooz.
  • Alag dashboard login: Har dukaan ka apna alag dashboard — kisi ka record kisi ko nazar nahi.
  • Urdu watermark: Customer ko pehle se wazeh — "ye mobile qiston par hai".

Net na ho to bhi kaam karega

Installock ka sab se bara faida: ye bina net ke bhi kaam karta hai. Due-date phone me saved hoti hai, is liye net band ho to bhi tareekh par auto-limit lag jata hai. Remote Lock/Unlock ka hukum net aate hi lag jata hai — koi hukum zaya nahi hota.
