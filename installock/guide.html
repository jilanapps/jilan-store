🔐 Installock — Dukaandaar Guide

Qiston par beche gaye mobile ka lock system (v1.3) — Roman Urdu guide
📌 Ye kya hai: Installock wo mobile dukaandaaron ke liye hai jo phone qiston (installments) par bechte hain. Har phone par ye app Device Owner ban kar rehti hai: qist ki due date guzar jaye to phone khud-b-khud limited ho jata hai — sirf bank/payment apps (JazzCash, Easypaisa waghera) chalti hain, baqi sab band. Dukaandaar ka PIN lagte hi full access wapas.

1️⃣ Do darjay ka PIN system (Two-Tier Admin)

RoleKaunKya kar sakta hai
⭐ Master AdminDr AsifHar phone unlock kar sakta hai, due date badal sakta hai, dukaan ka PIN change kar sakta hai — sab kuch.
🏪 DukaandaarHar mobile shop ka malikSirf apni dukaan ke phones: unlock, next due date, bank apps — apne phone par.

Setup ke waqt Master PIN (Dr Asif ka) aur Dukaan PIN dono dale jate hain. Dono me se koi bhi PIN lock khol deta hai. Master PIN Dr Asif har dukaan ko privately batate hain — ye guide me nahi likha.

2️⃣ Zaroori shart (pehle samajh lein)

⚠️ Seedhi baat:
  • Har phone par factory reset + QR scan zaroori hai — sirf APK install karne se ye system kaam NAHI karega (ye Android ki security hai, hamari marzi nahi).
  • QR scan ke waqt phone ko WiFi/internet chahiye (sirf ek dafa, APK download ke liye).
  • Uske baad SIM ho ya na ho, internet ho ya na ho — lock/due-date check poori tarah phone ke andar chalta hai. Koi SMS, koi server, koi network dependency nahi.
  • Factory reset karne se management khatam ho jata hai (dobara QR provisioning zaroori). Qist DUE (overdue) hone par Settings wala reset option khud band ho jata hai; dues clear (nayi due date) par wapas khul jata hai. Recovery-mode (power+volume) reset nahi roka ja sakta — ye Android ki had hai.
  • Phone par asal test abhi UNCONFIRMED hai — pehle ek test phone par azmayein, phir customer ko dein.

4️⃣ App me setup (har phone par, ek dafa)

Provisioning ke baad Installock kholein — setup wizard khulega:

  1. Dukaan ka naam aur rabta number likhein.
  2. Dukaan PIN set karein (dobara likh kar confirm).
  3. Master PIN likhein — ye Dr Asif se lein (har phone par ek jaisa).
  4. Qist ki due date chunein — date picker ya "30 din baad" button.
  5. Bank apps chunein — jo apps limited mode me chalengi (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank apps).

📱 IMEI app khud parh kar save kar leti hai — lock screen, dukaandaar panel aur record me nazar aayega.

5️⃣ Roz ka kaam — ye sab khud hota hai

  • 🔔 Phone par hamesha notification rehta hai: "Ye mobile qisto par hai" + lock screen par dukaan ka banner.
  • 💧 Watermark (v1.3+): screen par har waqt halki patti nazar aati hai — "Ye mobile qisto par hai — Jilan Apps". Gahak ko reminder rehta hai aur wo phone aage kisi ko bech nahi sakta (kharidar watermark dekh kar samjhega). Qist mukammal (Phone Release) par watermark band ho jata hai. Master PIN se watermark ka text change bhi ho sakta hai.
  • ⏰ Due date guzarte hi phone khud limited ho jata hai (check har ~6 ghante, reboot aur app khulne par bhi).
  • 🔒 Limited mode me calls NAHI, SMS NAHI, general network band — sirf bank/payment apps + lock screen. Gahak sirf qist ada kar sakta hai.
  • 🔓 Qist milte hi dukaandaar apna PIN lagaye → full access wapas + agli due date set kare.
  • 📤 "Record Share Karein" se phone ka record (IMEI, due date, status) WhatsApp par bhejein ya dashboard me import karein.
  • 🛡️ Sakht Mode (panel me): ON karne se Settings app chhup jati hai — gahak factory reset ka option ya app data clear nahi kar sakta. Zaroorat par panel se wapas ON/OFF.

6️⃣ Dashboard (record system)

💻 Installock Dashboard kholein — laptop ya mobile browser me chalta hai.

  • Master (Dr Asif) apne Master PIN se login → sab dukaanein, sab phones, IMEI list, due dates, overdue highlight.
  • Dukaandaar apne dukaan PIN se login → sirf apni dukaan ke phones.
  • App ke "Record Share Karein" wala text paste karke import karein, ya manually add karein. JSON backup download bhi hai.
⚠️ Dashboard v1 me sirf record hai — phone ko control nahi karta. Door se (internet par) lock/unlock ke liye server chahiye jo v2 me ayega. v1 me lock/unlock har phone par PIN se hota hai.

7️⃣ Ehem hadayat (honest limits)

  • Plain APK install Device Owner nahi ban sakta — factory reset + QR lazmi hai. Testing ke liye fresh phone par adb shell dpm set-device-owner com.jilanapps.installock/.InstallockAdmin use ho sakta hai.
  • Factory reset = management khatam (dobara provisioning). Recovery-mode reset nahi roka ja sakta.
  • Due-date check system clock par hai — gahak agar phone ki tareekh peeche kar de to check late ho sakta hai (v2 me server-time check ayega).
  • PIN bhool jayein to sirf factory reset + dobara provisioning rasta hai (ya Master PIN yaad ho to us se khol lein).
  • Device Owner active rehte app uninstall nahi ho sakti.
  • Emergency call button best-effort hai (phone ke dialer par munhasir).
  • Phone par asal test UNCONFIRMED — customer ko dene se pehle ek phone par poora flow azma lein.
