🔒 Privacy Policy

What we collect
Nothing. Jilan Photo & Video Compressor processes all files locally on your device using your browser's built-in image engine. No photos, videos, or personal data are uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.
Your files
Original files are never modified or overwritten. Compressed copies are created as new files in the current session only and are removed when you clear history or close the app. Saving a file writes it to your device's Downloads folder (or your chosen location) only when you tap Save.
Permissions
The app only asks to read the media files you explicitly select, and to save files you choose to keep. In the Android app these use the standard system file picker and Downloads folder.
Metadata
For your privacy, compressed photos are re-encoded without EXIF metadata (location, camera info). This is intentional.
