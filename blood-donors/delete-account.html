Delete Your Account

Basti Dawa Blood Society (BDBS)

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Basti Dawa Blood Society app.
  2. Go to the Account tab and log in.
  3. Open My Profile and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm. Your login account and your linked donor record will be deleted.

If the app asks you to log in again first, log out, log in once more, and then delete — this is a security check by Firebase.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, email bastidawabloodsociety@gmail.com from the email address or phone number on your account and ask for your account to be deleted. The admin will delete it and confirm back to you.

You can also contact the Founder & Chief Executive, Muhammad Asif Javaid, on WhatsApp: 0306-9140867.

What gets deleted

Your login account, your profile, and your linked donor record (including your public donor listing and certificates). Membership cards issued by the society are managed by the admin — ask the admin if you also want a membership record removed.
