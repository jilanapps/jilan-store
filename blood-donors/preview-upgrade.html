PSCA Virtual Blood Bank — Punjab Safe Cities Authority

English

The Punjab Safe Cities Authority (PSCA) Virtual Blood Bank is a system that helps connect patients in Punjab who need blood with voluntary blood donors.

Its main purpose is to help provide timely blood for emergency, operation, delivery, thalassemia and other patients.

How to get blood

  1. Call 15 from any mobile phone in Punjab.
  2. Tell the call operator that you need blood.
  3. Select Option 4 for the Virtual Blood Bank service.
  4. Tell the patient’s required blood group.
  5. Tell the patient’s city and hospital.
  6. Provide your contact number and the details of the need.
  7. Get guidance about contacting the relevant donors and their availability.

Information needed for the patient

Keep this information ready before calling:

  • Patient’s full name
  • Required blood group (A+, B+, O+ etc.)
  • How many units of blood are needed?
  • Hospital name and city
  • Ward or relevant department
  • Blood type: Whole Blood, RBCs, Platelets or Plasma
  • Patient attendant’s active mobile number

What happens next?

  • The PSCA team searches its registered donor list.
  • Possible donors are found according to blood group and the patient’s location.
  • The relevant donors are helped to contact the patient’s family; in some cases a conference call is also arranged.
  • The donor goes to the hospital blood bank and donates blood.
  • The hospital does the donor’s medical screening and the patient’s blood cross-match.

Please note: the Virtual Blood Bank helps in finding a donor; blood testing and transfusion are the hospital’s job.

Donor registration

If someone wants to donate blood voluntarily:

  1. Open the PSCA official website.
  2. Go to Volunteer Registration.
  3. Enter your name, father’s name, CNIC and date of birth.
  4. Enter your mobile number and district.
  5. Enter your full address and select your blood group.
  6. Provide disease-related information and submit the form.
  7. The PSCA team will verify the information and, after verification, include the donor in the network.

Emergency Helpline: 15 • Virtual Blood Bank WhatsApp: 0317-1000015 • Email: bloodbank@psca.gop.pk • Website: PSCA official website

This service is available 24/7. Patient requests and donor registration are free; the hospital’s own tests or other charges may be separate.

Note for Basti Dawa Blood Society: register all BDBS registered donors with PSCA Virtual Blood Bank too — it can further strengthen the donor network for needy patients in Multan, Basti Malook and other areas of Punjab.

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for Blood Donors

English

1. Donor Registration

  • Every donor’s full name, mobile number and blood group should be recorded.
  • The record should be saved with the donor’s consent.
  • The date of previous blood donation should be recorded.

2. Donor Eligibility

  • The donor’s age should generally be 18 to 65 years.
  • Weight should be at least 50 kilograms.
  • Hemoglobin and blood pressure should be according to the prescribed medical standards.
  • The donor should be physically healthy.
  • Final approval will be given by the authorized blood bank staff.

3. Before Donating Blood

  • Complete a good night’s sleep.
  • Eat a proper meal.
  • Drink adequate water.
  • Do not donate blood on an empty stomach.
  • Give correct information about your medicines and illnesses.

4. During Blood Donation

  • Blood should be donated only at an authorized blood bank.
  • A new and sterile needle should be used.
  • The donor’s full consent should be obtained.
  • Trained medical staff should collect the blood.

5. After Donating Blood

  • Rest for at least 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Drink water or juice.
  • Avoid heavy work and strenuous exercise for a few hours.
  • If you feel dizzy or weak, inform the medical staff.

6. Interval Between Donations

  • After a normal Whole Blood Donation, generally keep a gap of at least 3 months.
  • Medical eligibility must be re-checked every time.
  • Intervals for platelets and plasma donations are different.

7. Donor Privacy

  • The donor’s personal information should be kept safe.
  • Do not share anyone’s mobile number or medical information without permission.
  • The decision to donate or not is the donor’s own right.

8. Society’s Responsibilities

  • No money should be taken from any donor in exchange for blood donation.
  • Proper contact should be arranged between the patient and the donor.
  • Buying and selling of blood should be discouraged.
  • The patient should be referred to an authorized blood bank.
  • A record of every donation should be kept.

Our Pledge: We will give top priority to the respect, health, consent and privacy of blood donors.

BASTI DAWA BLOOD SOCIETY • Chief Executive: Muhammad Asif Javaid

Note: This is a draft of the basic SOPs for the society. Final medical rules will be set according to Pakistan’s relevant blood transfusion laws, Punjab’s rules and the authorized blood bank’s policy.

🩸 Benefits of Blood Donation

English

Blood donation is a great act of human service. It is not only a means of saving a needy person’s life, but it also promotes love, sympathy and the spirit of humanity in society.

15 Important Benefits of Blood Donation

  1. Saving a precious life: Your donation can give a person a new life.
  2. Helping thalassemia children: Such children regularly need blood.
  3. Helping pregnant women: In case of heavy bleeding during delivery, blood can save a life.
  4. Helping accident patients: In road accidents and other emergencies, blood is extremely important.
  5. Helping surgery patients: Blood may be needed during major operations.
  6. Helping cancer patients: Some patients need blood and platelets during treatment.
  7. Initial health check: Before donating blood, blood pressure, pulse and hemoglobin are checked.
  8. Reduction in iron levels: Donating blood reduces the amount of iron in the body, which can be beneficial for some people.
  9. Mental satisfaction: Taking part in saving a life brings happiness and peace.
  10. Serving humanity: Blood donation is service to humanity above colour, race, language and religion.
  11. Promoting the spirit of volunteering: It encourages others to donate blood too.
  12. Blood bank availability: It helps maintain blood stocks in hospitals.
  13. Social unity: Blood donation activities bring people closer to each other.
  14. Help in emergencies: Blood available on time can prove extremely valuable in any emergency.
  15. A chance to save lives: One donated blood can be divided into different components and used to treat more than one patient.

Precautions Before Donating Blood

  • Complete a good night’s sleep.
  • Eat a proper meal; do not donate blood on an empty stomach.
  • Drink adequate water.
  • Get your hemoglobin checked before donating.
  • Do not donate blood in case of fever, illness or severe weakness.
  • Rest a while after donating.
  • Always donate blood at an authorized blood bank.

How Often Can Blood Be Donated?

Generally a gap of about 3 months is kept between Whole Blood Donations; however, the final interval depends on the blood bank’s medical standards and the donor’s health.

  12. بلڈ بینک کی دستیابی: ہسپتالوں میں خون کے ذخیرے کو برقرار رکھنے میں مدد ملتی ہے۔
  13. معاشرتی اتحاد: خون عطیہ کرنے کی سرگرمیاں لوگوں کو ایک دوسرے کے قریب لاتی ہیں۔
  14. ایمرجنسی میں مدد: بروقت دستیاب خون کسی بھی ہنگامی صورتحال میں انتہائی قیمتی ثابت ہو سکتا ہے۔
  15. زندگی بچانے کا موقع: ایک عطیہ شدہ خون کو مختلف اجزاء میں تقسیم کرکے ایک سے زیادہ مریضوں کے علاج میں استعمال کیا جا سکتا ہے۔

خون عطیہ کرنے سے پہلے احتیاطی تدابیر

  • اچھی نیند پوری کریں۔
  • مناسب غذا کھائیں، خالی پیٹ خون عطیہ نہ کریں۔
  • پانی کا مناسب استعمال کریں۔
  • عطیہ کرنے سے پہلے ہیموگلوبن چیک کروائیں۔
  • بخار، بیماری یا شدید کمزوری کی صورت میں خون عطیہ نہ کریں۔
  • عطیہ کرنے کے بعد کچھ دیر آرام کریں۔
  • خون ہمیشہ مستند بلڈ بینک میں عطیہ کریں۔

خون کتنے عرصے بعد عطیہ کیا جا سکتا ہے؟

عام طور پر Whole Blood Donation کے درمیان تقریباً 3 ماہ کا وقفہ رکھا جاتا ہے، تاہم حتمی وقفہ بلڈ بینک کے طبی معیار اور ڈونر کی صحت کے مطابق ہوتا ہے۔

ہمارا پیغام: خون کا عطیہ صرف چند منٹ لیتا ہے، لیکن کسی کے لیے پوری زندگی بن سکتا ہے۔ آپ کا دیا ہوا خون کسی ماں کو زندگی دے سکتا ہے، کسی بچے کے چہرے پر مسکراہٹ لا سکتا ہے، کسی باپ کو اس کے خاندان کے پاس واپس لا سکتا ہے، کسی خاندان کو بکھرنے سے بچا سکتا ہے۔ آئیے! خون عطیہ کریں، زندگی بچائیں اور انسانیت کا فرض ادا کریں۔
