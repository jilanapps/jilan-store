Privacy Rule

What the public can see — and what stays secret.

Who Sees What

Public: only name, phone number and photo.

Secret — admin only: everything else (CNIC, age, education, email, address, father name, designation, ID, blood group).

A QR scan shows only photo, name, phone and VERIFIED MEMBER.

Blood Request Flow

How a blood request reaches a donor.

From Request to Donation

  1. A blood request comes to the admin first.
  2. The admin messages matching donors (same blood group) via WhatsApp from inside the app.
  3. The donor connects directly and the donation is arranged.
  4. Donor phone numbers stay admin-only — they are never shown publicly.
