Privacy Policy

Basti Dawa Blood Society (BDBS)

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

Basti Dawa Blood Society (BDBS) is a blood donation society working under the supervision of Basti Dawa Welfare Organization, based in Basti Malook, District Multan, Punjab, Pakistan. This policy explains what data our blood donor app collects, why, and how it is protected.

Contact: bastidawabloodsociety@gmail.com • Founder & Chief Executive: Muhammad Asif Javaid — WhatsApp 0306-9140867

Data we collect

  • Donor registration: name, father name, donor number, photo (optional), CNIC, blood group, age, phone number, address, disease, last blood donation date, total units donated.
  • Blood requests: patient name, father name, age, CNIC (optional), blood group needed, hospital, disease, phone number, address.
  • Membership: name, father name, designation, member ID, CNIC, blood group, age, education, phone number, email, address, photo, card issue and expiry dates.
  • Accounts: name, email address or phone number, and password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see your password).
  • Activity and certificates: activity posts and donor appreciation certificates generated by the admin.

Donors must be aged 18 to 65. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

What is public and what is private

Public (visible to anyone using the app): donor name, blood group, city, last donation date and availability; blood request first name, blood group needed, hospital, city, request date, phone number and progress; member name, phone number and photo.

Private (visible only to the society admin after login): donor phone number, CNIC, address, disease, father name; member CNIC, age, education, email, address, father name, designation, ID number and blood group; full blood request details.

Donor phone numbers and CNIC numbers are never shown publicly.

How we use data

Data is used only to connect blood donors with patients who need blood, to issue membership cards and donor certificates, and to publish society activity. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or other third parties.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore Database and Firebase Authentication) and is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Only the society admin account can access private records.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open the app, go to Account, log in, and tap Delete My Account. This deletes your login account and your linked donor record. You can also ask for deletion by emailing bastidawabloodsociety@gmail.com or through the Delete Account page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
