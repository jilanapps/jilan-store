Privacy Policy

Jilan Online Shopping Store ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Order details: When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, city, and delivery address — only to process and deliver your order.
  • WhatsApp orders: Orders placed via WhatsApp are handled through WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

How We Use Data

  • To process, deliver, and confirm your orders (Cash on Delivery).
  • To contact you about your order status.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Order details are shared only with our delivery partners to complete your delivery.

Data Storage

Order records are kept securely and only for as long as needed for order processing and accounting.
