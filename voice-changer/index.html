⚙️ Ye kaise kaam karta hai?

Mic → pitch shifter (granular DSP, aapki setting ke semitones) → resonance EQ (2–4 kHz presence) → speaker. Sab kuch aapke phone par hota hai — koi server nahi, koi upload nahi. Ye AI voice conversion NAHI — sirf signal processing hai, is liye awaz robotic/unnatural lag sakti hai.
