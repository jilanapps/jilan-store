Android 7.0+ · Free · No account needed

Features

  • Deleted message recovery - see messages even after the sender deletes them
  • Deleted detection - deleted messages are highlighted in red
  • View Once log - view-once photo/video arrivals are recorded with sender and time
  • Sender filter - browse messages by contact
  • WhatsApp filter - one-tap WhatsApp-only view
  • 100% private - everything stays on your phone, no cloud, no account

Setup Guide

  • Install the app and open it
  • Tap Enable Access
  • Find Jilan Msg Saver in the list and turn it ON, then confirm

The app reads incoming notifications to save messages. It cannot read old messages from before installation.
