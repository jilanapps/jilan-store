Privacy Policy — Virtual Trade Pro

Effective: October 15, 2026 • v1.0 • by Jilan Apps Official

What this app is

Virtual Trade Pro is an educational paper-trading simulator. All money in the app is virtual. There is no real-money trading, no deposits, no withdrawals, and no connection to any broker, exchange, or bank account.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Your virtual portfolio, trade history, and settings are stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage). Nothing is sent to our servers — we operate no servers for this app.

Third-party price data

The app fetches public cryptocurrency prices from the CoinGecko public API (api.coingecko.com). That is a standard HTTPS request for market data; we do not send them any of your information. See CoinGecko's privacy policy.

Advertising & analytics

This version contains no ads and no analytics.

Children

The app is a general educational tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
