Features

  • Auto call recording - incoming and outgoing calls recorded automatically
  • Recording list - browse by number, date and duration
  • Play, share, delete - full control over every recording
  • Audio source options - microphone, voice communication, voice call
  • 100% private - everything stored only on your device

Setup

  1. Install the APK and open the app
  2. Tap Grant Permissions and allow all requests
  3. Keep Auto-record calls ON
  4. Make or receive a call - it will be recorded automatically

Important limitation

On Android 10 and above, Google blocks third-party apps from recording the other side of a call directly. This app records through the microphone - for both sides to be heard clearly, please turn on speakerphone during the call. Please follow your local laws about call recording consent.
