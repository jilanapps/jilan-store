Privacy Policy — Jilan Online Shopping Store

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Jilan Online Shopping Store ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or password. Specifically:

  • No account data: The app works without sign-up or login.
  • Local app data only: Your cart items, wishlist, and recently viewed products are stored only on your own device (browser/app local storage). We never receive this data.
  • WhatsApp ordering: When you tap "Order on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-written order message. That message is sent by you through your WhatsApp account to our helpline number (0300 7379385). Any personal details you include in that chat (name, address, phone number) are shared by you voluntarily via WhatsApp and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • No tracking SDKs: The app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics trackers.
  • Internet permission: The app uses the internet only to load the product catalogue, images, and prices from our website.

2. How We Use Information

Order details you send us via WhatsApp are used solely to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information to anyone.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your on-device data (cart, wishlist) never leaves your phone. Order chats sent via WhatsApp are stored in your WhatsApp and ours, protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption.

4. Children's Privacy

The app is a general shopping app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your Rights

You may clear the app's data at any time from your phone settings to delete locally stored cart/wishlist data. To request deletion of order chat records, contact us on WhatsApp at 0300 7379385.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this same web address and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
