Download APK (v1.0)

Android 7.0+ · Free · No account needed · Works offline

Features

  • PIN lock - set a 4-digit PIN to protect your apps
  • Lock any app - WhatsApp, Gallery, Settings, anything
  • PIN gate - locked apps ask for your PIN on launch
  • Session unlock - stays unlocked until screen off
  • Search - quickly find apps in the list
  • Change PIN - update your PIN anytime from the app
  • 100% private - everything stays on your phone, no cloud, no account

Setup Guide

  • Install the app and open it
  • Set your 4-digit PIN
  • Tap Enable Protection and turn on Jilan App Lock in Accessibility settings
  • Turn ON the lock switch for any app you want to protect
