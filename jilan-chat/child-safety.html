Jilan Chat — Child Safety Standards

Developer: Jilan Apps Official · Effective date: 22 November 2026

Our standards

Jilan Chat has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Using the app to create, share, request or promote such content, or to groom or exploit minors, is strictly prohibited.

  • Jilan Chat is intended for adults (18+) chatting with friends and family.
  • Users cannot be found by name search, and nobody can start a chat without the other person accepting a chat request.
  • Every user can block any other user at any time.

Reporting and enforcement

Report any child-safety concern to jilanappsofficial@gmail.com or WhatsApp 0300-7379385, with the @username or Gmail of the account involved.

We review reports promptly, permanently remove offending accounts and their content, and report CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Pakistan's FIA Cyber Crime Wing, as required by law.
