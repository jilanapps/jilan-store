Jilan Chat — Privacy Policy

Developer: Jilan Apps Official · Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

Jilan Chat ("the app") is a private messaging app made by Jilan Apps Official, Basti Malook, District Multan, Pakistan. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Contact: jilanappsofficial@gmail.com · WhatsApp: 0300-7379385

2. Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Gmail address, name and Google account ID through Firebase Authentication. If you create a username account, we store your chosen username, display name and a password that is handled by Firebase Authentication (we never see your password in plain text).
  • Profile. Your display name, optional "About" text, optional profile photo and optional cover photo (small compressed images).
  • Messages and media. Text messages, and the photos, voice notes, short videos (up to 1 minute), small documents, stickers, polls and status updates you choose to send, are stored in compressed form in Google Cloud Firestore so they can be delivered to the people you are chatting with. Channel posts are stored the same way and can be read by anyone who follows the channel link.
  • Location (optional). Only when you choose to share your location or live location in a chat, the app reads your phone's location and stores it with that message so the other person can see it. Live location is updated about every 30 seconds while the app is open, for the time you choose (15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours), and you can stop it at any time. Location is never read in the background or when you are not sharing.
  • Notification token. To show new-message and incoming-call notifications when the app is closed, the app gets a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and saves it on your account. It is used only to deliver your notifications.
  • Chat information. Who you chat with, chat requests (accept/decline), groups you belong to, blocked users, message time, delivered/read status, and your online / last-seen time.
  • Call signalling. To start a voice or video call, the app exchanges connection information (call offer/answer and network candidates) through Firestore. Call history (who called, when, and whether it was answered) is shown in your Calls tab.
  • Device permissions. Camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo or video, record a voice note, or make a call. Bluetooth is used only for the optional Nearby chat feature. Location is used only when you share it. Fingerprint is checked by your phone for the optional app lock; we never receive it. Screen capture is used only when you share your screen in a video call, after Android asks for your consent.
  • Translate (optional). When you tap Translate on a message, only that message text is sent to a free translation service (Google Translate, or MyMemory as a fallback) to get the translation. Nothing is sent unless you tap it.

We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, background location, location history or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

3. How your information is used

  • To create your account and let other people find you by your exact username or exact Gmail address (search by display name is not available).
  • To deliver your messages, photos, voice notes and calls to the people you choose.
  • To show delivery ticks, online status and call history.
  • To keep the service safe (for example blocking, and one account per device).

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

4. Where your data is stored and security

  • Data is stored on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google, in the asia-south1 region. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google.
  • Important: messages are private between the participants by access rules, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on Google's servers in a form that the service infrastructure can technically read. Please do not send highly sensitive information such as passwords or bank details.
  • Calls are peer-to-peer (WebRTC). Audio and video travel directly between the two phones, encrypted by WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP). Google's public STUN servers are used only to help the two phones find each other; call audio/video is not stored by us.
  • Nearby chat (Bluetooth) messages go directly from phone to phone, are kept only in memory while the Nearby screen is open, and are never uploaded.

5. Data retention

  • Photos and voice notes are kept in compressed form with the chat. We are adding automatic deletion of chat media after 3 days; until that is live, media stays until the chat or account is deleted. Media you save to your own phone's gallery stays on your phone.
  • Status updates disappear after 24 hours.
  • Text messages and your account remain until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.

6. Delete your account and data

To request deletion of your Jilan Chat account and all its data, contact us from the Gmail address or with the username of your account:

  • Email: jilanappsofficial@gmail.com (subject: "Jilan Chat account deletion")
  • WhatsApp: 0300-7379385

Please include your @username or Gmail address. We will verify the request and delete your account, profile, messages you sent, chat lists, chat requests, call history and media within 30 days. Messages already delivered to other people may remain in their chat until they delete them. No data is kept after deletion, except where the law requires it.

7. Children

Jilan Chat is not intended for children under 13 and is meant for adults chatting with friends and family. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

  • You can change your display name, About text and profile photo at any time in Profile.
  • You can block any user, and decline chat requests from people you do not know.
  • You can deny camera, microphone, Bluetooth, location or notification permission in your phone's settings; only the related feature will stop working.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
