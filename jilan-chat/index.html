About Jilan Chat

Jilan Chat

Version 4.15

Private chats with friends and family. No ads, no tracking, no price.

What you can do

  • Messages, replies, reactions and voice notes (up to 1 minute)
  • Voice and video calls
  • Photos and small documents (up to 200 KB)
  • Status updates: photo, text or voice, gone after 24 hours
  • Groups with private invite links, and polls
  • Channels you can follow by link
  • Location sharing, including live location
  • Nearby chat over Bluetooth, without internet

Good to know

  • Large videos and big files are not supported yet. Photos are compressed before sending.
  • Your chats are private between you and the other person. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • For calls, both people need the app open.
