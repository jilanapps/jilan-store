Community Guidelines

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Jilan Reels is a clean, family-friendly place for short videos. Please keep it that way. These rules apply to videos, captions, hashtags, sounds, comments, messages and profiles.

Not allowed
  • Nudity and sexual content — no naked or half-naked bodies, sexual acts, sexual dancing or poses, or sexual jokes.
  • Immoral or vulgar content — no vulgar words, gaali, or content meant to be shameful or indecent.
  • Violence and danger — no real fights, gore, weapons used to threaten, or dangerous stunts others may copy.
  • Hate and bullying — no attacks on anyone for their religion, sect, caste, language, gender or looks; no harassment.
  • Anything involving children that is sexual, harmful or exploitative. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • Scams and spam — no fake offers, gambling, betting or "earn money" schemes, and no misleading links.
  • Other people's content — only post videos you made or have the right to share.

Games and movies

Game clips, trailers and game hashtags (for example #gta6) are fine, as long as the clip itself has no nudity or sexual content.

How we keep it clean

  • Before posting, you confirm your video has no nudity, sexual or violent content.
  • Captions, hashtags and sound names with blocked words cannot be posted.
  • When three different users report a video, it is hidden automatically until our team reviews it.
  • Our team removes videos that break these rules and can ban accounts. Serious cases may be reported to the authorities.

Report a problem

Tap ⋯ on any video → Report video, and choose a reason. You can also report accounts and comments, and block anyone.
