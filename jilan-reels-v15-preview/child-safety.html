Community & Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Jilan Reels has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Our standards

  • Jilan Reels is for users 18 and older.
  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors is banned and is removed as soon as we find it.
  • Accounts that post or share such content are banned permanently.
  • We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Pakistan's FIA Cyber Crime Wing.

How to report

  • In the app: tap ⋯ on a video → Report video → Child safety. Accounts and comments can be reported the same way.
  • By email: jilanappsofficial@gmail.com (subject: Child safety).

Our response

Reports are reviewed by our team. Content can be hidden at once and deleted, and the account banned. You can also block any account so they cannot follow you or comment on your videos.
