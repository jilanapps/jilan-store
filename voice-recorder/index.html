Features

  • One-tap recording - record from your microphone in AAC quality
  • Live timer - see recording duration while you record
  • Playback - play any recording right inside the app
  • Delete - remove recordings you no longer need
  • Share - send recordings via WhatsApp, email, or any app
  • 100% private - everything stays on your phone, no cloud, no account

How to Use

  • Install the app and grant microphone permission
  • Tap Record to start, tap Stop to save
  • Tap a recording to select it, then Play, Delete, or Share
