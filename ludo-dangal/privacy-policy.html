💪 Privacy Policy — Ludo Dangal

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Ludo Dangal ("the Game") is published by Jilan Apps Official. We respect your privacy.

Data collection

The Game itself collects no personal data: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. However, the Game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and ad interaction data (for example, which ads are shown or tapped) to provide and measure advertising. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data usage information.

On-device storage

Game settings (such as sound on/off) are stored only on your own device using the app's private storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

The Game requests no sensitive permissions. Internet access is used to check for game updates, to load the latest game version from our website when available (the bundled offline copy is used otherwise), and to load advertisements from Google AdMob. Approximate location may be used by AdMob for ad targeting, as described above.

Children

The Game is a family board game suitable for all ages. The Game itself collects no data from anyone, including children; advertising data described above is handled by Google AdMob under Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
