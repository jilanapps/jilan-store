🔒 Privacy Policy — Jilan AI Chat

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your API Key

Jilan AI Chat works with your own free Google Gemini API key. The key is stored only on your device (in the app's local storage). It is never sent to us, never uploaded anywhere, and never shared. It is sent only to Google's servers (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) when you ask the AI a question — that is how the AI answers you.

Your Chats

Your chat history is stored only on your device. We do not see, collect, or store your conversations on any server.

Messages Sent to Google

When you send a message, it is transmitted to the Google Gemini API so the AI can generate a reply. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. Please do not share highly sensitive personal information with the AI.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, no sign-up — we don't know who you are.
  • No analytics, no tracking.
  • No ads — the app contains no advertising.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or other phone data.

Data Deletion

You can delete all chats anytime from the app menu ("Clear all chats"), remove your API key from Settings, or simply uninstall the app — everything is erased with it.

Children

The app is a general-purpose AI chat tool and is not directed at children under 13.
