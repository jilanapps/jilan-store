Privacy Policy - Jilan Translator

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Jilan Translator ("the app") is published by Jilan Apps Official, Basti Malook, Multan, Pakistan. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

No account, no tracking

The app does not require an account or sign-in. It contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Text you translate

To translate text, the app sends the text you enter to a third-party online translation service (Google Translate web endpoints, with MyMemory as a backup) over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and shows you the result. Please do not translate sensitive personal information. These services process the text under their own privacy policies.

Photos you scan

When you use the Scanner, the photo you take or choose is resized on your phone and sent over HTTPS to the OCR.space text recognition service to read the text in it. The app does not keep a copy of the photo on any server. The camera permission is used only when you tap "Take photo".

Data stored on your phone

Your translation history, chosen languages and dark mode setting are stored only on your phone. You can delete the history at any time in the app (History → Clear all, or Settings → Clear history). Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

Offline dictionary and text-to-speech

The English-Urdu dictionary works fully offline. The "Listen" feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
