Privacy Policy — Jilan Assistant

Effective: 17 October 2026 · Jilan Apps Official

1. Your data stays on your phone

Jilan Assistant stores your tasks, reminders, goals, prayer times and chat history only on your own device (local storage). We do not run any server and we do not collect, transmit or sell your data.

2. Microphone (voice commands)

When you tap the mic button, your speech is converted to text on your device (Android speech recognition). Voice audio is processed by your phone's speech service; we do not record or upload your voice.

3. Contacts (calling / WhatsApp by name)

If you use a voice command like "call Ammi" or "WhatsApp Ahmed", the app reads your phone's contacts on-device only to match the spoken name to a phone number. Contact names and numbers never leave your phone.

4. Phone calls

The "call" command places a phone call directly from your device using Android's dialer. No call data is collected.

5. Notifications, alarms, timers & prayer times

Reminders, timers and prayer-time alerts are scheduled with Android's AlarmManager on your device. Prayer times you enter are stored only on your phone.

6. Flashlight

The "light jalao" voice command turns your phone's torch on/off using the camera flash hardware. The app does not take photos or record video.

7. Camera (photo command)

The "photo lo" command opens your phone's own camera app to take a picture; the photo is saved by the camera app to your gallery normally. Jilan Assistant does not access or keep the photo.

8. Weather (your city)

If you ask for the weather, the city name you entered in Settings is sent to the free Open-Meteo weather API (no account, no key) to fetch the current temperature. Only the city name is sent, only when you ask.

9. Notes

Quick notes are stored only on your device.

10. AI chat (your own Gemini key)

The AI chat uses your own free Google Gemini API key, which you paste yourself. The key is stored only on your device and your messages are sent only to Google's Gemini API (subject to Google's privacy policy). We never see your key or messages.

11. Accessibility Service (call disconnect)

The optional "call band karo" voice command uses an Accessibility Service that clicks your phone's End Call button — only when you give the command. It does nothing else, reads nothing else, and collects no data. You must enable it manually in Settings → Accessibility → Jilan Assistant.

12. No ads, no tracking

Jilan Assistant shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.
