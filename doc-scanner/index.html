Jilan Doc Scanner

Turn your camera into a document scanner.

Android 7.0+ · Free · No account needed

Features

  • Camera scanning - capture documents with your phone camera
  • Auto edge detection - finds the document borders automatically
  • Manual crop - drag the corners to fine-tune the scan area
  • Filters - color enhance or crisp black & white
  • Multiple pages - scan as many pages as you need
  • PDF export - save everything as one PDF in Downloads
  • 100% private - everything stays on your phone, no cloud, no account

How to Use

  • Install the app and open it
  • Tap Scan Document and allow camera access
  • Fit the document in the frame and tap SCAN
  • Drag the corners if needed, pick Color or B&W, then tap Save Page
  • Repeat for more pages, then tap Export as PDF
  • Find your PDF in the Downloads folder
