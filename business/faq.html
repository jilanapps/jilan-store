Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers — no hidden charges, no false promises.

How much does an app cost?

On Fiverr our packages are $100 (basic), $180 (standard) and $300 (premium), depending on features and revisions. For direct orders on WhatsApp, we give a custom quote after understanding your business and feature list — you only pay for what you actually need.

How long does it take?

Typically 7 to 21 days depending on the package: simple catalog apps in about a week, bigger apps with ordering, booking or khata features in two to three weeks. We confirm the exact timeline before starting, and keep you updated during the build.

Will my app be on the Google Play Store?

Yes — we prepare the store-ready app file and guide you through publishing. Publishing needs your own Google Play developer account (a one-time $25 fee paid to Google, not to us). We walk you through every step of the upload.

Do you build iPhone (iOS) apps?

No — Android only. Our apps are built for Android phones, which is what the vast majority of customers in our market use. If you need an iPhone app, we're not the right fit and we'll tell you honestly.

What do you need from me to start?

Just four things: 1) your business type and name, 2) your logo and any photos (or we work without them), 3) the feature list — catalog, WhatsApp ordering, booking, khata, and 4) your WhatsApp number for order notifications. No technical knowledge needed from your side.

Do the apps work offline?

It depends on the features. Catalogs, menus and saved records can work fully offline. Features like WhatsApp ordering need internet at the moment of ordering — which is normal for any ordering system. Ask us about your specific features and we'll tell you exactly what works offline.

How do payments work? Is my money safe?

Orders through Fiverr or Upwork are protected by the platform — your payment is held safely until the work is delivered and approved. For direct WhatsApp orders, we agree on clear milestones in writing before starting, so both sides know exactly what happens when.

Do I need technical knowledge to run the app?

No. Every app is made to be simple: it installs like any normal app on an Android phone, and we explain everything in plain words. If you can use WhatsApp, you can run your app.
