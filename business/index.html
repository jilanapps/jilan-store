Paid Solution

Installock

Installock helps mobile shops recover installments on time. The shop gets its own dashboard login, customer record and qist schedule, and the customer's Android phone connects to the system. When the qist is paid on time, the phone runs normally; when a qist is missed, the phone becomes limited — and it fully reopens as soon as the qist is received.

JazzCash and Easypaisa payments keep working even while the phone is limited, every shop keeps a separate record and dashboard, and after the final qist the phone belongs fully to the customer.

Paid solution — price on request. Available for Android phones.
