Privacy Policy — Jilan Snake Zone

Last updated: October 15, 2026
Developer: Jilan Apps Official

In short: Jilan Snake Zone collects no personal data. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and nothing is ever sent to us.

1. Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or location data.

2. On-device storage

Your high scores, best length, and selected snake skin are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage) so the game can show your leaderboard and remember your preferences. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

3. Internet use

The game loads its game content from our website (jilanapps.github.io) when you are online, and falls back to a built-in offline copy when you are not. No personal data is transmitted during this process.

4. Permissions

The app requests Internet access solely to load the game content described above. It does not access your contacts, files, camera, microphone, or location.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact Jilan Apps Official at jilanappsofficial@gmail.com.
