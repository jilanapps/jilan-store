Jilan Bill Maker

Simple bill maker for small shops. Make bills, share them on WhatsApp, and track daily sales. Works offline, no login needed.

  • Add items with quantity and price - total is calculated automatically
  • Discount, grand total, and saved bill history
  • Share any bill via WhatsApp with one tap
  • Today's total sales at a glance
  • Built-in update check
