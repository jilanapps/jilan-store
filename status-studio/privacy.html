Privacy Policy — Jilan Status Studio

By Jilan Apps Official. Last updated: 2 November 2026.

1. No account required

You can use all features without registering or signing in.

2. Your designs stay on your device

Designs, photos and text you create are stored locally on your phone. We do not upload your photos or designs to any server.

3. No data collection

The app does not collect personal data, does not track you across apps, and does not sell any information. The app declares no permissions in its manifest, uses no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no account system. Photo picking uses the Android system picker, so the app only receives the photos you choose. Finished designs are saved to your Pictures folder only when you export them, and shared only when you tap Share and choose a destination in Android's Sharesheet.

4. No ads in this version

This version contains no advertising SDKs and no ad units.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's Sharesheet opens and you choose where the image or text goes. The app does not post to WhatsApp or any social network by itself. Sharing is via the Android Sharesheet — this is not direct WhatsApp integration. Two share choices are offered on each item:

  • As picture: a real image of the design (1080×1920) is rendered and shared through the Sharesheet. This also saves a copy in your Gallery (Pictures/Jilan Status Studio), because it reuses the gallery export path — delete it from your Gallery if you do not want to keep it.
  • As text: only the text is shared through the Sharesheet, with no image attached.

6. Islamic content

Quranic verses are shown with their surah:ayah reference (quran-uthmani text via api.alquran.cloud, fetched 2026-10-31 and bundled in the app; Urdu translation by Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry). Hadith items carry their book and number (Sahih al-Bukhari No. 1, also Sahih Muslim No. 1907). Original reflections, Islamic Poetry couplets and Funny Quotes are labelled as original and are never presented as Quran or Hadith, and no reference is invented for them. No Durood text is pre-filled — the Durood template is blank for users to type their own text.

7. Delete your data

Settings > Delete all local designs removes your saved designs from this device. Gallery exports are your files and can be deleted from your Gallery.

8. Children

The app is a general creative tool. Parents should supervise children's use of sharing features.

9. Contact

Jilan Apps Official. Use the Share / Feedback option in Settings to send feedback from your device.
