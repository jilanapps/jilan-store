Jilan Reels

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what Jilan Reels (by Jilan Apps Official) collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account: username and password (stored by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), or your Google account email and name if you sign in with Google.
  • Public profile: name, username, optional bio and small profile photo, follower and following counts.
  • Content: videos you post (stored on Cloudinary), captions, hashtags, comments, likes and follows.
  • Safety data: reports you send, accounts you block.
  • Device permissions: camera and microphone are used only while you record a video; files are read only when you choose a video or photo.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, location or advertising ID, and the app shows no ads.

How we use it

To run the app (show videos, profiles, comments), keep the community safe (review reports, block abuse), and fix problems. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database) — Firebase privacy
  • Cloudinary (video storage and delivery) — Cloudinary privacy
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of the app pages)

Public information

Your profile, videos, captions and comments can be seen by anyone, including people without an account and people who receive a share link.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a video removes it from the app at once; the file is then removed from Cloudinary by our team. Deleting your account removes your profile, videos, comments, likes, follows and sign-in. See Delete your account. Reports you sent may be kept for safety review.

Children

Jilan Reels is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using the app, contact us and we will remove the account.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, and database security rules stop users from changing other people's data.
