Features

  • Live RTSP streaming - watch your Hikvision DVR cameras in real time
  • Multi-camera grid - 4, 9 or 16 cameras on one screen
  • Tap for fullscreen - tap any camera for a full-screen view
  • Easy DVR setup - just enter IP, port, username and password once
  • Main / Sub stream - use sub-stream for smooth multi-camera viewing
  • 100% private - connects directly to your DVR, no cloud, no account

Setup Guide

  • Install the app and open it
  • Enter your DVR IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.64), port (554), username (usually admin) and password
  • Tap Save & Connect - your cameras appear
  • Tap the grid button (4) to switch between 4, 9 and 16 camera views
  • Tap any camera for fullscreen. Long-press also opens fullscreen

Finding Your DVR Details

  • IP address: check the sticker on your DVR, or use the Hikvision SADP tool on a computer on the same network
  • Port: RTSP port is 554 by default
  • Stream URL format: rtsp://user:pass@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101
  • Your phone must be on the same WiFi network as the DVR (or use port forwarding for remote access)
