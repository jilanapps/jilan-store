Privacy Policy — Jilan App Store

Jilan App Store by Jilan Apps Official is a directory/listing app. It shows Dr Asif's own apps with download links.

Data collection

No data is collected. The app does not ask for, store, or transmit any personal information. It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Downloads

Tapping a download button fetches the app's APK file from jilanapps.github.io. Standard network download behavior of your device/browser applies.

Installed-app check

To show "Installed" or "Update" badges, the app reads the list of installed package names on your device. This information never leaves your device.
